Partner with Team Bumblebee

Team Bumblebee is building an open-source autonomous rover in Bangladesh, with PCB manufacturing completed and the project now moving into mechanical prototyping and system integration. We are seeking engineering partners across manufacturing, hardware, software, and financial infrastructure to support the ongoing development.

Current Partners
Organizations supporting this project

Manufacturing is already underway. The following organizations are active partners in the development of Team Bumblebee's open-source rover.

Why partner with us
What this collaboration involves

Team Bumblebee operates as an open engineering project. Every design decision, component selection, and test result is documented publicly.

Fully open-source engineering

All schematics, KiCad files, STEP files, firmware, and build documentation are published on GitHub. Partner contributions are permanently credited in that record.

Long-term hardware development

The rover platform is designed for modular expansion. Active development continues across mechanical, electronics, firmware, and autonomy layers.

Student engineering in an underserved region

This project demonstrates that open-source hardware development is replicable with locally available components, advancing robotics education in Bangladesh.

Engineering transparency

Partner-supplied components and tools are documented with real performance data, integration notes, and honest evaluation — not promotional content.

Rapid Prototyping
Local iteration capability

Large structural components are handled by JLC3DP. Local desktop printing is needed for the rapid design iteration that active robotics development requires.

Why local prototyping matters

Outsourcing every design iteration to an overseas service introduces multi-week delays that slow engineering progress significantly. A local printer enables same-day validation of design changes.

✓ Motor mount geometry validation
✓ Sensor bracket iterations
✓ Electronics enclosures
✓ Cable management components
✓ Repair and replacement parts
✓ Alternative mechanical design testing

What we are seeking

  1. 1
    Desktop FDM 3D printerCompatible with PLA and PETG. Used as an engineering development tool for prototype iteration.
  2. 2
    PLA filamentFor prototyping and non-structural components.
  3. 3
    PETG filamentFor mechanical components requiring higher durability.
  4. 4
    Replacement nozzles and maintenance suppliesTo maintain consistent output quality throughout development.

We are open to evaluating different FDM platforms. All equipment will be documented through the engineering process and credited across project documentation.
